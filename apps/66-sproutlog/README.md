# SproutLog

**Operations for in-home daycares — the daily sheet parents actually read, tuition that pays itself, and the licensing binder that's always inspection-ready.**

## The enemy

The paper daily sheet that dies in a diaper bag. Licensed family
childcare providers (4–12 kids in a home) run a real regulated
business between diaper changes: handwritten daily sheets nobody
reads, tuition chased by text on Fridays, attendance and meal counts
reconstructed the night before a licensing visit or a food-program
claim. Every admin hour is an hour after the kids leave. SproutLog
kills the paper sheet: tap-logged moments become a parent digest that
actually gets read, tuition runs on autopay, and the compliance
binder writes itself from the same taps.

## Who pays

- Licensed in-home/family childcare providers (the buyer and daily
  user — often a solo operator).
- Small group homes with one assistant.
- Parents receive digests and pay invoices; they never pay SproutLog.

## MVP feature list

1. **Children + enrollment** — child profiles (allergies, authorized
   pickups, emergency contacts), schedules (MWF/full-week), tuition
   rate per child.
2. **Tap logging** — the provider's one-thumb day: arrival/departure
   (the attendance timestamps licensing wants), meals (breakfast/AM
   snack/lunch/PM snack with components for food-program claims),
   naps (start/end), diapers/potty, incidents, photos.
3. **The daily digest** — at pickup time, one email per family: the
   day compiled into plain sentences with photos ("Slept 12:40–2:10.
   Ate all of lunch. Two diaper changes."), not an app they must
   check.
4. **Tuition autopay** — weekly/monthly invoices generated from
   enrollment, Stripe autopay on file, late-fee schedule, past-due
   flags; the Friday text, retired.
5. **Licensing logs** — attendance registers, meal counts (CACFP-
   shaped), nap checks, incident reports with signatures — all
   derived from the taps, exportable per state-inspection format.
6. **Incident reports** — structured form (what/when/action taken),
   parent signature capture on pickup, PDF copy to both parties.
7. **Ratio awareness** — the header shows current headcount vs. the
   license limit; over-ratio moments are flagged honestly in the log.
8. **Parent roster** — contacts, payment status, digest preferences.
9. **Menus** — week menu templates that pre-fill meal logs (CACFP
   component checklists).
10. **Exports** — everything to CSV/PDF; the inspection binder in one
    click.

## Competitive landscape

Brightwheel and Procare own the center-based market (per-student
pricing, feature sprawl built for directors with front desks);
HiMama/Lillio similar. Family-childcare-specific tools are dated or
abandoned. The wedge: SproutLog is shaped for one adult with a phone
in one hand — tap targets, digest-not-app parent communication, and
CACFP-shaped meal logs that make the food-program claim a print job
instead of a reconstruction.
