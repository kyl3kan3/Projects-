Your tracker thinks you're 26 and regular.

Mainstream cycle apps are built on 28-day math that collapses the moment cycles turn irregular — which is the defining feature of perimenopause. And the menopause apps that do exist track symptoms but not treatment, so "did the new dose help?" is answered by vibes. MenoCompass is built the other way up.

Irregular is the design, not the bug

No predicted periods, ever. Cycle gaps are the unit — "day 47 since last period" is a data point here, not an error state.

Treatment is a first-class object

Patches, gels, sprays, tablets, cyclical progesterone. Every dose change becomes a dated marker on every chart: what changed, and what changed after.

Nothing leaves your phone

No account. No analytics. No servers of ours. Airplane mode is the proof — the whole app keeps working, forever.
